how to use it

/bdaypheus Start a birthday invite

Opens the invite form. Pick the channel (and any ping groups) to invite people from, the birthday person, their birthday, and a planning channel. Bdaypheus can create a new private channel for you, or you can pick one you already made.

  • Edit the invite message and the list of people before sending.
  • Only one person can start invites for each birthday. If someone already did, Bdaypheus tells you who to DM.
  • Creating a channel needs your account linked first (see /link-bdaypheus).
/send-reminders Nudge people who haven't answered

DMs everyone who hasn't answered yet a link to their invite. If you started more than one birthday, you pick which one from a list.

/send-reminders @birthday person

Skips the list and reminds people for that person's birthday.

/link-bdaypheus Link your Slack account

Links your Slack account so Bdaypheus can create planning channels as you and invite people from private channels. You can also link from the home page.

Why it's needed · What the token can do

/unlink-bdaypheus Revoke your token

Revokes your token with Slack and deletes it from the bot. Same as "Revoke my token" on the home page.

@bdaypheus Ping the bot

Replies with a message only you can see, pointing you here.
