Bdaypheus

Annoyed at being added to random birthday channels for no reason? Well with Bdaypheus (bday + orpheus), you're now able to send a mass invite to people and get them added to the proper channel easily.

Link your Slack account

Needed for a few functions, check here for why.

Log in with Slack

You'll sign in with Hack Club Auth and authorize Slack, read the warning before to know what it can do!

Connected

Your Slack account is linked to Bdaypheus. It only uses your token when you run /bdaypheus/ the parts that require user tokens.

Revoking deletes your token and tells Slack to cancel it, same as running /unlink-bdaypheus.

How it works

  1. Run /bdaypheus and pick who to invite, whose birthday it is, and when.
  2. Bdaypheus then will send out an invite DM to everyone you designate.
  3. If they accept the invite, they'll automatically get added to the planning channel!

An explanation of the bot and its commands is listed here.

Questions?

Check out why linking is needed and the warning page for what your token can and can't do. If you have any more (or something's broken), ask in #bdaypheus-dev or ping @iamalive on Slack.
