bdaypheus

smol warning for ya

Linking your Slack account to this bot creates a personal Slack access token tied to your account.

What it can do

  • Read the name and full member list of any channel you are in. Bdaypheus does this for the channel you pick under "Channel to invite from" in /bdaypheus, public or private.
  • Create a private planning channel as you when you pick "Create a new private channel" in /bdaypheus, then add the bot to it.

What it can't do

  • No reading messages, no posting as you, nothing else.

The exact permissions

  • channels:read and groups:read: see the name and member list of public and private channels you're in.
  • groups:write: create private channels and invite people to them. Bdaypheus only uses it to create your planning channel and add itself.

Slack's groups:write technically covers more than that (like renaming or archiving private channels you're in). Bdaypheus never does those, and the code is open source if you want to check.

Where it's kept

Your token is stored on the server Bdaypheus runs on, and only used when you run /bdaypheus. The bot admin gets a DM whenever someone links or unlinks.

How to revoke it

  1. Run /unlink-bdaypheus in Slack. This revokes the token and deletes it from the bot.
  2. Or remove it yourself: Slack → your apps → Bday Bot → Remove.

After revoking, Bdaypheus asks you to link again next time it needs your token. Why it needs one at all.
